Privacy

Clear information about your data.

This page explains how this website and the separately operated Salon Booking Automation handle information.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This website

This website provides information about web design, booking-system, and support services offered by Raul Pino. It does not use account registration, advertising cookies, or an embedded contact form. If you choose to contact Raul by email, your email provider and the receiving email service process the message, and the message may be retained as needed to respond and keep ordinary business records.

Salon Booking Automation

Salon Booking Automation is a limited-access workflow used to help operate a salon appointment system. It is separate from this informational website. Access is currently restricted to approved test users while the system is being tested.

Information the workflow may process

  • Appointment details that a customer submits, such as name, contact information, selected service, date, and time.
  • Google Sheets records used to organize appointments and operational status.
  • Google Calendar availability and event information used to check times and manage appointments.
  • Email information needed to prepare or send appointment-related messages when an authorized operator enables that function.
  • Limited technical records used for security, troubleshooting, and preventing duplicate actions.

How information is used

Information is used only to check availability, receive and manage booking requests, create or update appointment records, send authorized appointment communications, troubleshoot the workflow, and protect it from unauthorized or duplicate activity.

Google user data

Google account access is used only when an approved operator authorizes the automation. The workflow uses that access to operate the connected appointment records, calendar, and approved communications. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. Access is limited to the operator, the business for which the system is configured, and service providers needed to run the workflow.

Service providers

The workflow may rely on Google Workspace and Apps Script, GitHub, and Cloudflare to store code, host pages, route authorized requests, and operate connected services. Each provider handles information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

Appointment and operational records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for scheduling, business records, troubleshooting, and legal obligations. Reasonable access controls, restricted credentials, request validation, and operational safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information under the operator’s control, subject to recordkeeping and legal requirements. An approved Google test user can also revoke the application’s account access through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to pino.raul2@gmail.com.

Before public OAuth use: this notice will be reviewed against the final Google permissions, the salon business’s role, and the production data-retention policy. The current wording is intentionally limited to the testing setup observed on November 7, 2026.
